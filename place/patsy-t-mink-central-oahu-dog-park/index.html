Patsy T. Mink Central Oʻahu Dog Park

A 12,800 sq ft fenced lawn with a distinctive parallelogram design at the makai end of Patsy T. Mink Central Oʻahu Regional Park, opened in 2021.

📍 94-580 Lumiauau St, Waipahu, HI 96797 (makai end of Central Oʻahu Regional Park, where Paiwa St ends)

🐶 Dog rules

Off-leash within the fence with separate big-dog and small-dog areas; drinking fountains, irrigation/sprinklers, and ADA-accessible paths.

Daily during daylight hours
