Are dogs allowed at Keokea Park?

Yes — Keokea Park is open to leashed dogs. Maui County requires dogs be leashed (10 ft max leash) at all public parks and beaches by default — there's no per-park designation list like Oʻahu's, it's the county-wide baseline rule. Pick up after your dog and check posted signs.
