Kepaniwai Park & Heritage Gardens

A shaded riverside park at the entrance to Iao Valley, with heritage gardens honoring Maui's ethnic history — pavilions, streams, and lawns good for a leashed walk.

📍 870 Iao Valley Rd, Wailuku, HI 96793

🐶 Dog rules

Maui County requires dogs be leashed (10 ft max leash) at all public parks and beaches by default — there's no per-park designation list like Oʻahu's, it's the county-wide baseline rule. Pick up after your dog and check posted signs.

Are dogs allowed at Kepaniwai Park & Heritage Gardens?

Yes — Kepaniwai Park & Heritage Gardens is open to leashed dogs. Maui County requires dogs be leashed (10 ft max leash) at all public parks and beaches by default — there's no per-park designation list like Oʻahu's, it's the county-wide baseline rule. Pick up after your dog and check posted signs.
