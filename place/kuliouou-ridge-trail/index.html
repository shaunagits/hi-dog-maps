Kuliʻouʻou Ridge Trail

A challenging 5-mile round-trip Na Ala Hele ridge hike through ironwood and Cook pine forest to a Koʻolau summit with panoramic windward views.

📍 Trailhead at end of Kalaʻau Pl, Honolulu, HI 96821 (street parking in residential neighborhood)

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs allowed under Na Ala Hele rules; keep dogs leashed — the upper stairs and summit section are steep and exposed.

🕐 Hours

Daily during daylight hours

✨ Amenities

  • Street parking
  • Shade on lower trail
  • Covered rest shelters

Dog rules on Oʻahu

Common questions

Are dogs allowed at Kuliʻouʻou Ridge Trail?

Yes — Kuliʻouʻou Ridge Trail is open to leashed dogs. Leashed dogs allowed under Na Ala Hele rules; keep dogs leashed — the upper stairs and summit section are steep and exposed.

What are the hours at Kuliʻouʻou Ridge Trail?

Daily during daylight hours

Where is Kuliʻouʻou Ridge Trail?

Trailhead at end of Kalaʻau Pl, Honolulu, HI 96821 (street parking in residential neighborhood) — East Honolulu, Oʻahu. Coordinates 21.3036, -157.7242.
