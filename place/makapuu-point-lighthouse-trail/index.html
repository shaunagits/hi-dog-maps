Makapuʻu Point Lighthouse Trail

A paved 2-mile round-trip trail climbing to the Makapuʻu lighthouse overlook, with whale watching in winter and sweeping views of the southeastern coast.

📍 Makapuʻu Lighthouse Rd off Kalanianaʻole Hwy, Waimanalo, HI 96795 (Kaiwi State Scenic Shoreline parking lot)

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs allowed; the paved path gets very hot at midday, so go early or late to protect paws. Clean up after your dog.

🕐 Hours

Gates daily 7:00 AM - 7:45 PM (summer); closes 6:45 PM rest of year

✨ Amenities

  • Parking
  • Paved trail
  • Scenic overlooks
  • No water or restrooms
