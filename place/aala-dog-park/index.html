ʻAʻala Dog Park

An urban fenced dog park at the edge of ʻAʻala Park near Chinatown and downtown Honolulu.

📍 280 N King St, Honolulu, HI 96817 (ʻAʻala Park, Chinatown)

🐶 Dog rules

Off-leash inside the fenced enclosure with separate small and large dog areas; leash required elsewhere in ʻAʻala Park.

🕐 Hours

Daily 6:00 AM - 9:00 PM

✨ Amenities

  • Fenced
  • Small & large dog areas
  • Benches
  • Street parking

Dog rules on Oʻahu

Leashed dogs are allowed only at parks and beaches the City & County of Honolulu has specifically designated — there is no blanket permission.
