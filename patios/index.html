Common questions

How many dog-friendly restaurant patios are there in Hawaiʻi?

This map lists 34 across 2 islands: 18 on Oʻahu, 16 on Maui.

Why are there no dog-friendly patios on Hawaiʻi Island?

Hawaiʻi County Code § 4-3-5 makes it unlawful to bring a dog to any establishment where food is sold or displayed, restaurants included, with only service and law-enforcement dogs exempt. The state Department of Health food-code change of 24 August 2025 that legalised dogs in outdoor dining areas is a food-safety rule and does not repeal a county animal-control ordinance. Individual businesses may welcome dogs anyway, but the county code still says otherwise.
