Mokuleʻia Beach Park

A remote, uncrowded stretch of white sand on the far North Shore across from Dillingham Airfield; on the city's on-leash dog park list.

📍 68-919 Farrington Hwy, Waialua, HI 96791 (across from Dillingham Airfield)

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs allowed; the area is undeveloped and quiet, ideal for long on-leash beach walks. Strong currents in winter.
