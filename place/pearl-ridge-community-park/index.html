Pearl Ridge Community Park

A neighborhood community park in ʻAiea near Pearlridge with lawns, a playground, and walking paths. Convenient green space now open to leashed dogs.

📍 98-960 Moanalua Road, ʻAiea, HI 96701

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs are allowed under the City on-leash park designation; leash required and owners must clean up.
