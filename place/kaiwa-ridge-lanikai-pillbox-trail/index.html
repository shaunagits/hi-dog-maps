Kaʻiwa Ridge (Lanikai Pillbox) Trail

A short but steep 1.8-mile out-and-back ridge hike to two WWII pillboxes with sweeping views over Lanikai, the Mokulua islands, and Kailua Bay. One of the most popular quick-summit hikes on the Windward side.

📍 Kaʻiwa Ridge Trailhead, 265 Kaelepulu Drive, Kailua, HI 96734

🐶 Dog rules

Dogs are welcome but must be kept on a leash at all times; bring water as there is no shade and it gets hot on the exposed ridge.

🕐 Hours

Sunrise to sunset (daily)

✨ Amenities

  • Street parking
  • Ocean views
  • No restrooms
  • No water

Are dogs allowed at Kaʻiwa Ridge (Lanikai Pillbox) Trail?
