Dog parks on Kauaʻi 1

The opposite of Maui: Kauaʻi County parks and beaches prohibit dogs entirely except by permit. The places that work are narrow, specific carve-outs.

  • Off-leash Dog park Verify first Kauai Humane Society Dog Park Lihue · Kauaʻi A fenced off-leash dog park on the Kauai Humane Society's grounds, with separate areas for large and small dogs.
