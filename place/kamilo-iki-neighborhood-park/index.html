Kamilo Iki Neighborhood Park

A neighborhood park in the Kamiloʻiki valley of Hawaiʻi Kai with sports fields, a skate park, and open lawns. A designated on-leash park in East Honolulu.

📍 7750 Hawaii Kai Drive, Honolulu, HI 96825

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs are permitted under the City on-leash park designation; leash required and clean up after your dog.
