Kilohana Dog Park

A small fenced dog area in Kihei. Unlike Maui County's other dog parks, it isn't built to official off-leash-park standards, and there's no dedicated nearby parking.

📍 17 Kilohana Dr, Kihei, HI 96753

🐶 Dog rules

Off-leash inside the fence, but explicitly called out by local sources as not an official/standard dog park — verify current condition and access before visiting. Maui County requires dogs be leashed (10 ft max leash) at all public parks and beaches by default — there's no per-park designation list like Oʻahu's, it's the county-wide baseline rule. Pick up after your dog and check posted signs.

Daily, 7:00 AM - 8:00 PM
