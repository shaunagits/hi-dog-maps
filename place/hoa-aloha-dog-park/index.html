Hoa Aloha Dog Park

A 10,000 sq ft fenced neighborhood dog park serving the Salt Lake and Moanalua communities.

📍 5080 Ala Loke St, Honolulu, HI 96818 (Salt Lake, at Hoa Aloha Neighborhood Park)

🐶 Dog rules

Off-leash within the fence with designated large-dog and small-dog areas; dogs must be leashed outside the enclosure.

🕐 Hours

Daily 5:00 AM - 8:00 PM; closed Tuesday mornings until noon

✨ Amenities

  • Fenced
  • Small & large dog areas
  • Benches
  • Street parking

Dog rules on Oʻahu

Leashed dogs are allowed only at parks and beaches the City & County of Honolulu has specifically designated — there is no blanket permission.
