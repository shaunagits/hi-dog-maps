Puna Trail (to Hāʻena / Shipman Beach)

An easy 2.9-mile coastal trail on the old government road out to Hāʻena (Shipman) Beach. Bikes, horses and hunting are prohibited. Frequently very muddy. High surf and dangerous shorebreak at the beach; car break-ins reported at the trailhead.

📍 Nā Ala Hele trailhead, Puna, Hawaiʻi Island

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs are permitted — this trail carries the Nā Ala Hele “Dog on leash” designation from the state Division of Forestry & Wildlife. Dog policy on this system is per-trail, not per-reserve: neighbouring trails in the same forest reserve can differ, so don't assume it carries over.

🕐 Hours

Open daily, dawn to dusk

Dog rules on Hawaiʻi Island

A hybrid, and the reason you can't generalise from another island: leashed dogs are fine in county parks on a 6-foot lead, but are banned outright from every county beach park, even leashed.
