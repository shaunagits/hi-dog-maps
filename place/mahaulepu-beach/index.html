Mahaulepu Beach

A wild, undeveloped beach near Poʻipu on privately-owned land (Grove Farm Company), with dunes considered a sacred, historic site.

📍 End of Poipu Rd, Koloa, HI 96756

🐶 Dog rules

This is private land, not a county or state beach — the Kauaʻi Humane Society lists it as dog-approved, but access depends on the landowner's gate schedule rather than a legal public right. Keep dogs leashed and respectful of the site's cultural significance.

🕐 Hours

Gate opens early morning, closes in the evening (varies seasonally)
