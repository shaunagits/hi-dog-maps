Kahakapao Loop Trail (Makawao Forest Reserve)

A shaded loop trail through eucalyptus and Cook pine forest in Upcountry Maui's Makawao Forest Reserve, recommended by the Maui Humane Society for leashed dog hikes.

📍 Kahakapao Rd, Makawao, HI 96768

🐶 Dog rules

Maui County requires dogs be leashed (10 ft max leash) at all public parks and beaches by default — there's no per-park designation list like Oʻahu's, it's the county-wide baseline rule. Pick up after your dog and check posted signs.
