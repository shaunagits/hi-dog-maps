ʻAweoweo Beach Park

A quiet, local neighborhood beach park in Waialua with a sandy shore, grassy lawn, and playground. Far less crowded than the main North Shore beaches.

📍 68-197 Au Street, Waialua, HI 96791

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs are allowed under the City on-leash park designation; keep dogs on leash and pick up waste.
