Kalapaki Beach

A calm, sandy beach beside Nawiliwili Harbor in Lihuʻe, popular for leashed dog walks along the shoreline.

📍 Nawiliwili, Lihue, HI 96766

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs are commonly allowed on the beach/shoreline itself, though the adjacent county-maintained Nawiliwili Park does not allow dogs — that's the state-shoreline-vs-county-park split seen elsewhere in Hawaiʻi. Kauaʻi's default rule is stricter than Oʻahu's or Maui's: dogs are prohibited in county parks and county beach parks except by permit. This spot is an exception where leashed dogs (generally 6-8 ft max) are specifically allowed under state or trail-system rules, not the county default — check posted signs, since even similar nearby trails can have different rules.
