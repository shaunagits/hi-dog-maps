Waʻahila Ridge Trail (Waʻahila Ridge State Recreation Area)

A Na Ala Hele ridge trail starting in a Cook pine grove above St. Louis Heights, rolling along the ridge between Manoa and Palolo valleys (~4.8 miles round trip).

📍 Top of Ruth Pl via St. Louis Heights Dr, Honolulu, HI 96816

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs welcome under state park and Na Ala Hele rules; picnic areas at the trailhead make it an easy shorter outing too.

🕐 Hours

Gates daily 7:00 AM - 7:45 PM (summer); closes 6:45 PM rest of year
