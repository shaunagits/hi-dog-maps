        About HI Dog Maps

        HI Dog Maps is a hand-curated guide to every place across Hawaiʻi where you can actually bring your dog — off-leash dog parks, leash-friendly parks and beaches, hiking trails, and dog-welcoming restaurant patios. It currently covers Oʻahu and Maui, with more islands planned.

        Every spot here comes from official designations and verified lists, not a scrape or a guess — sites with unclear or nuanced rules are flagged ⚠️ Verify before visiting rather than presented as certain.
