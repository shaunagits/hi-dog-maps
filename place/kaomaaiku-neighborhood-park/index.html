Kaomaʻaiku Neighborhood Park

A roughly 10-acre Mililani Mauka park with sports fields, an inline hockey rink and open turf that recently gained extended daily hours. Added to the City's on-leash dog park list in February 2026.

📍 95-1000 Makaʻunulau St, Mililani, HI 96789

🐶 Dog rules

Dogs are welcome on a leash of six feet or less; keep pets off active sports fields and clean up after them.
