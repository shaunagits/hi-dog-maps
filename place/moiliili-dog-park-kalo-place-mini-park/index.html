Moʻiliʻili Dog Park (Kalo Place Mini Park)

A compact fenced dog park at Kalo Place Mini Park near the University of Hawaiʻi at Manoa.

📍 2705 Varsity Pl, Honolulu, HI 96826

🐶 Dog rules

Off-leash inside the fence with separate small and large dog sections; street parking is limited.

🕐 Hours

Daily 5:00 AM - 10:00 PM; closed Tuesdays 7:00 AM - 12:00 PM for maintenance

✨ Amenities

  • Fenced
  • Small & large dog areas
  • Benches
  • Limited street parking

Dog rules on Oʻahu
