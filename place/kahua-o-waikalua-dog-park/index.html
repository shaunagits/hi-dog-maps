Kahua O Waikalua Dog Park

Windward Oʻahu's first off-leash dog park, opened at Kahua O Waikalua Neighborhood Park in Kaneʻohe.

📍 45-231 Kulauli St, Kaneʻohe, HI 96744

🐶 Dog rules

Off-leash inside the fence with separate large and small dog areas; benches, shade and drinking fountains provided.

🕐 Hours

Daily 7:00 AM - 7:00 PM; closed Thursdays 7:00 AM - 11:00 AM for maintenance

✨ Amenities

  • Fenced
  • Small & large dog areas
  • Water fountains
  • Shade
  • Benches
  • Parking
