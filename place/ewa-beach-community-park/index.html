Ewa Beach Community Park

An inland community park in Ewa Beach with sports fields, courts and a community center hosting an open market. A pilot site for the City's new dog-park signage and dog-waste bag dispensers.

📍 91-955 North Rd, Ewa Beach, HI 96706

🐶 Dog rules

Dogs must be leashed (six feet or less); owners must pick up after their pets using the provided waste bags.

🕐 Hours

Daily, sunrise to sunset

✨ Amenities

  • Sports fields
  • Courts
  • Community center
  • Waste bag dispenser

Dog rules on Oʻahu
