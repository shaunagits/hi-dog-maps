Common questions

Are dogs allowed at Wailea Beach Path?

Yes — Wailea Beach Path is open to leashed dogs. Maui County requires dogs be leashed (10 ft max leash) at all public parks and beaches by default — there's no per-park designation list like Oʻahu's, it's the county-wide baseline rule. Pick up after your dog and check posted signs.

What are the hours at Wailea Beach Path?

Open daily, dawn to dusk
