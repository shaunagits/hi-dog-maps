Kaloko-Honokōhau National Historical Park

Coastal trails and beaches just north of Kailua-Kona, where leashed dogs may even swim. Approach from the Honokōhau Harbor or Kaloko parking areas — the lava rock gets hot enough to burn paws.

📍 Kaloko-Honokōhau NHP, Kailua-Kona, HI 96740

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs (six feet maximum) are allowed on the trails and shoreline here. National Park Service rules apply rather than the county ordinance. Dogs are excluded from the sacred and archaeological areas — keep to the trails and picnic areas. The Kaloko and ʻAimakapā fishponds and all anchialine pools, including Queen's Bath, are closed to dogs.

Dog rules on Hawaiʻi Island

A hybrid, and the reason you can't generalise from another island: leashed dogs are fine in county parks on a 6-foot lead, but are banned outright from every county beach park, even leashed.
