Schuler Family Foundation & Jones Family Community Dog Park

Opened May 2025 at the Hawaiian Humane Society's Kosasa Family Campus, this members-only community dog park effectively replaces the society's former McInerny Dog Park in Moʻiliʻili, which is permanently closed.

📍 91-1945 Fort Weaver Rd, ʻEwa Beach, HI 96706 (Hawaiian Humane Society Kosasa Family Campus at Hoʻopili)

🐶 Dog rules

Off-leash for registered members only (registration is free, donations encouraged); separate small and large dog areas, play structures, gazebos and water.

🕐 Hours

Wed-Mon 11:00 AM - 6:00 PM; closed Tuesdays for maintenance

✨ Amenities

  • Fenced
  • Small & large dog areas
  • Water stations
  • Play structures
  • Gazebos/shade
  • Parking
  • Free membership required
