Common questions

How many dog-friendly beaches are there in Hawaiʻi?

This map lists 68 across 4 islands: 57 on Oʻahu, 6 on Maui, 2 on Kauaʻi, 3 on Hawaiʻi Island.

Can dogs go on Hawaiʻi state park beaches?

No. State-park beaches are closed to dogs on every island, leashed or not, service animals excepted. County beach rules vary enormously — Maui allows leashed dogs, Hawaiʻi Island bans them from county beach parks outright, Kauaʻi requires a permit, and Oʻahu allows them only at designated beaches.
