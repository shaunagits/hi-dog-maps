🦮 Off-leash Dog park Oʻahu

Ala Wai Dog Park

A 15,000 sq ft fenced dog run beside the Ala Wai Canal, the closest off-leash park to Waikiki.

📍 2444 Hihiwai St, Honolulu, HI 96826 (Ala Wai Community Park)

🐶 Dog rules

Off-leash inside the fence with separate large-dog and small-dog sections; water spigot on site, leash required outside.

🕐 Hours

Daily during daylight hours; closed Tuesday mornings for maintenance

✨ Amenities

  • Fenced
  • Small & large dog areas
  • Water spigot
  • Seating
  • Street parking

Dog rules on Oʻahu

Leashed dogs are allowed only at parks and beaches the City & County of Honolulu has specifically designated — there is no blanket permission.
