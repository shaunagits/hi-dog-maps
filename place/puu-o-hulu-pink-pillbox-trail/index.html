Puʻu O Hulu (Pink Pillbox) Trail

A steep 1.5-mile out-and-back climb to a chain of pink-painted pillboxes above Maʻili, with panoramic Leeward coast and ocean views. Quick but demanding, gaining roughly 700 feet.

📍 Kaukama Road Trailhead, Maʻili, Waianae, HI 96792

🐶 Dog rules

Dogs are welcome on a leash; the climb is steep and exposed with no shade or water, so it is best for fit dogs in cooler hours.

🕐 Hours

Sunrise to sunset (daily)

✨ Amenities

  • Roadside parking
  • Ocean views
  • No restrooms
  • No water
