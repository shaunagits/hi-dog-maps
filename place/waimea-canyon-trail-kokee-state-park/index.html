Waimea Canyon Trail (Kokeʻe State Park)

A trail into Waimea Canyon, "the Grand Canyon of the Pacific," inside Kokeʻe State Park.

📍 Kokeʻe Rd, Waimea, HI 96796

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs (6 ft max) are allowed on this trail and in the park's campground under official state park policy, but not in swimming areas, pavilions, or lodges. Kauaʻi's default rule is stricter than Oʻahu's or Maui's: dogs are prohibited in county parks and county beach parks except by permit. This spot is an exception where leashed dogs (generally 6-8 ft max) are specifically allowed under state or trail-system rules, not the county default — check posted signs, since even similar nearby trails can have different rules.
