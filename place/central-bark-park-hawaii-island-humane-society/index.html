Central Bark Park (Hawaiʻi Island Humane Society)

Two fenced, double-gated off-leash parks at the Humane Society's Animal Community Center above Kailua-Kona, with separate areas for small dogs (25 lb and under) and large dogs, plus a bone-shaped splash pad.

📍 78-6767 Māmalahoa Hwy, Holualoa, HI 96725

🐶 Dog rules

Off-leash inside the fenced enclosure. Run by the Hawaiʻi Island Humane Society and free to use, but dogs must be currently vaccinated and licensed. Maximum three dogs per adult, leashed entering and exiting, and no food, treats or toys inside. These are the only public off-leash dog parks on the island.

🕐 Hours

Mon, Wed, Fri–Sun 8 AM – 5 PM; closed Tuesday and Thursday

Dog rules on Hawaiʻi Island

A hybrid, and the reason you can't generalise from another island: leashed dogs are fine in county parks on a 6-foot lead, but are banned outright from every county beach park, even leashed.
