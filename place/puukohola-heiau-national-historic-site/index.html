Puʻukoholā Heiau National Historic Site

A National Park Service site above Kawaihae with a half-mile loop trail between the visitor centre, the massive 1791 heiau and the shoreline. It sits directly beside Spencer Beach Park — which is a County beach park where dogs are barred — making this the dog-friendly alternative next door.

📍 62-3601 Kawaihae Rd, Kawaihae, HI 96743

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs (six feet maximum) are welcome on the trails and shoreline. Dogs may not enter the heiau structures themselves. NPS rules apply here rather than the county ordinance, and the park runs a B.A.R.K. Ranger programme.

🕐 Hours

Grounds open daily; check NPS for visitor centre hours

Dog rules on Hawaiʻi Island

A hybrid, and the reason you can't generalise from another island: leashed dogs are fine in county parks on a 6-foot lead, but are banned outright from every county beach park, even leashed.
