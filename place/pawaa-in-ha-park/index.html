Pawaʻa In-Ha Park

A small urban park between Young and King Streets in the Pawaʻa area of central Honolulu, with lawn, benches and a play area. Recently designated on-leash for dogs.

📍 1461 Young St, Honolulu, HI 96814

🐶 Dog rules

Keep dogs on a leash no longer than six feet and clean up all waste.

🕐 Hours

Daily, sunrise to sunset

✨ Amenities

  • Open lawn
  • Benches
  • Playground
  • Street parking

Dog rules on Oʻahu

Leashed dogs are allowed only at parks and beaches the City & County of Honolulu has specifically designated — there is no blanket permission.
