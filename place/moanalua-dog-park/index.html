Moanalua Dog Park

A 1.3-acre fully fenced off-leash park inside Moanalua Community Park, one of the city's original and most popular dog parks.

📍 2900 Moanalua Rd, Honolulu, HI 96819 (Moanalua Community Park)

🐶 Dog rules

Off-leash allowed inside the fenced enclosure; dogs must be leashed to and from the gate. Water stations and waste bag dispensers provided.

🕐 Hours

Daily during daylight hours; closed Tuesday mornings for maintenance

Dog rules on Oʻahu

Leashed dogs are allowed only at parks and beaches the City & County of Honolulu has specifically designated — there is no blanket permission.
