Keʻehi Lagoon Beach Park

A large, flat waterfront park near the airport with huge open fields, canoe halau and shaded picnic areas; on the city's on-leash dog park list.

Dog rules on Oʻahu

Leashed dogs are allowed only at parks and beaches the City & County of Honolulu has specifically designated — there is no blanket permission.
