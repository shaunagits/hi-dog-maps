Dole Community Park

A small community park in the Papakolea/Punchbowl area above downtown Honolulu with a playground and open lawn. A neighborhood green space newly designated for on-leash dogs.

📍 602 Magellan Avenue, Honolulu, HI 96813

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs are allowed under the City on-leash park designation; leash and clean-up required.

🕐 Hours

5:00 AM - 10:00 PM (daily)

✨ Amenities

  • Street parking
  • Playground
  • Grassy areas
