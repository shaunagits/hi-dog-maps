Kaʻalawai Beach

A narrow local beach at the base of Diamond Head near Black Point, with good snorkeling on the reef and a mostly residential, low-key feel.

📍 Kulamanu Place (public access), off Diamond Head Road, Honolulu, HI 96816

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs are commonly permitted on this state-jurisdiction shoreline, which is not a City beach park and has no sanctuary designation. Not confirmed by a specific City ordinance, so verify posted signs on arrival.

🕐 Hours

Open daily, dawn to dusk (public shoreline access)
