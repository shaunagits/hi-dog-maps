Keopuolani Dog Park

A fenced off-leash dog park inside Kahului's large Keopuolani Park, one of Maui County's official dog parks.

📍 700 Halia Nakoa St, Kahului, HI 96732 (Keopuolani Park)

🐶 Dog rules

Off-leash allowed inside the fenced enclosure; dogs must be leashed to and from the gate. Maui County requires dogs be leashed (10 ft max leash) at all public parks and beaches by default — there's no per-park designation list like Oʻahu's, it's the county-wide baseline rule. Pick up after your dog and check posted signs.

🕐 Hours

Daily, 7:00 AM - 7:00 PM

Dog rules on Maui

The most permissive regime in the state: leashed dogs are welcome by default in Maui County parks and on county beaches, on a lead of 10 feet or less.
