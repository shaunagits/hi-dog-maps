Green World Coffee Farm

A working coffee farm and cafe set on seven acres of gardens with over 2,000 arabica trees, on the Kamehameha Highway between Wahiawa and the North Shore. Serves estate coffee and espresso drinks.

📍 71-101 Kamehameha Hwy, Wahiawa, HI 96786
