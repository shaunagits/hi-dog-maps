Kahiwelo Neighborhood Park

A quiet neighborhood park in the upper Makakilo (Kahiwelo) subdivision with open grass, a playground and sweeping views toward the coast. Newly designated on-leash for dogs in the City's February 2026 expansion.

📍 92-2100 Kulihi St, Kapolei, HI 96707

🐶 Dog rules

Dogs must be kept on a leash no longer than six feet at all times. Owners are required to pick up and dispose of all pet waste.

🕐 Hours

Daily 5:00 AM - 10:00 PM

  • Street parking
  • Scenic views
