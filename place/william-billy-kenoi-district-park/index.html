William “Billy” Kenoi District Park

A district park in Pāhoa, Puna, formerly known as Pāhoa District Park and renamed in November 2022 to honor former Hawaiʻi County Mayor William "Billy" Kenoi; it includes the Ginny Aste Skate Park, Pāhoa Aquatic Center, Pāhoa Neighborhood Facility, and Pāhoa Senior Center, and served as a hub for families displaced by the 2018 Kīlauea eruption.

📍 Pāhoa, HI 96778

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs are allowed. Hawaiʻi County Code § 4-4-29 permits dogs in County parks when kept on a leash no longer than six feet. Note that dogs are barred from County beach parks on this island entirely, even leashed — this is a regular county park, not a beach park.

🕐 Hours

Open daily, dawn to dusk (county park)
