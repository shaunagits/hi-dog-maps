Manoa Falls Trail

A lush 1.6-mile round-trip rainforest hike through bamboo and banyan to a 150-foot waterfall, one of Honolulu's most popular trails (Na Ala Hele).

📍 End of Manoa Rd (3860 Manoa Rd area), Honolulu, HI 96822 — paid lot at Paradise Park

🐶 Dog rules

Leashed dogs allowed under Na Ala Hele rules; trail is often muddy, and dogs must stay leashed and be cleaned up after.

🕐 Hours

Daily during daylight hours; paid parking lot roughly 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM

✨ Amenities

  • Paid parking
  • Restrooms at trailhead
  • Shade
  • Waterfall

Dog rules on Oʻahu
