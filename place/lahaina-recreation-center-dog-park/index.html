Lahaina Recreation Center Dog Park

A fenced off-leash dog park at Lahaina's recreation/civic center complex.

📍 280 Shaw St, Lahaina, HI 96761

🐶 Dog rules

Off-leash allowed inside the fenced enclosure; dogs must be leashed to and from the gate. Maui County requires dogs be leashed (10 ft max leash) at all public parks and beaches by default — there's no per-park designation list like Oʻahu's, it's the county-wide baseline rule. Pick up after your dog and check posted signs. Lahaina town was heavily affected by the August 2023 wildfire — confirm this facility is open and unchanged before visiting.
